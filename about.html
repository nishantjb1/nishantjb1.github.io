About

I am a dual degree student at IIT Bombay, finishing a B.Tech in Electrical Engineering and an M.Tech in Controls and Computing with dual minors in Robotics and Machine Learning.

Growing up in Mumbai, I was surrounded by a world that rarely behaved according to plan. Roads were unpredictable, traffic patterns changed by the minute, and people somehow navigated this complexity through countless decisions made in real time. Long before I knew the language of robotics, control theory, or machine learning, I was fascinated by a simple question: How do intelligent agents make good decisions in an uncertain world?

As I explored engineering, I found myself increasingly drawn to mathematics, control systems, optimization, artificial intelligence, and robotics. What fascinated me was not just building systems that could act autonomously, but understanding how they could do so safely, reliably, and with guarantees, even when faced with uncertainty.

Today, my research sits at the intersection of robotics, autonomous systems, controls, and AI. I am particularly interested in developing safety guarantees for learning-enabled autonomy and designing decision-making frameworks for multiple agents operating in complex, uncertain environments. To me, the most exciting challenge in robotics is not teaching machines to make decisions. It is ensuring that those decisions remain trustworthy when the world behaves in unexpected ways.
