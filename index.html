Hi, I’m Nishant.

Dual degree in Electrical Engineering and Controls, IIT Bombay

I work on the parts of autonomy that have to be argued rather than demonstrated.

Whether a controller can be certified to hold a system inside a set it must never leave, whether a plan stays feasible once uncertainty and other decision makers enter the problem, and what it costs to establish either.

Curriculum vitae

My master’s thesis builds a validated simulation testbed for dense lane-less Indian traffic, a setting where the lane abstraction that most traffic models rest on does not hold and interaction between heterogeneous, non-compliant agents dominates the dynamics. That problem set my current direction, towards the stochastic and risk aware versions of the guarantees I had been using, and towards the verification of learned components sitting inside a control loop.
