Curriculum Vitae

I work on safety guarantees for autonomous systems that operate under uncertainty and alongside other decision makers.

Most of what I do is first principles. Reachability and reach avoid guarantees, control barrier and Lyapunov functions, model predictive control under constraints, and the optimisation theory underneath all three. My master's thesis builds a validated simulation testbed for dense lane-less Indian traffic, a setting where the lane abstraction that Western traffic models rest on simply fails, and where interaction between heterogeneous and non-compliant agents dominates the dynamics.

That problem is what set my current direction. Deterministic certificates are the wrong instrument for dense unstructured interaction, so I want to work on the stochastic and risk aware versions of them. Chance constrained and distributionally robust safety, and the verification of learned components sitting inside a control loop.

I am applying for PhD positions beginning in autumn 2027, in safe control, formal guarantees for learning enabled autonomy, and multi-agent decision making under uncertainty.

Network-Optimal Decentralised Bilevel Optimisation

A single-loop decentralised bilevel optimiser combining Chebyshev-accelerated consensus with optimistic gradient tracking. Hybrid Reach-Avoid MPC with Backstepping Control

With Shankar Deka, Aalto University.

A hybrid bounded-control reach-avoid MPC backstepping controller that addresses the infeasibility of finite-horizon MPC by integrating exponential control barrier functions for safety and reachability. Feasibility of the optimiser is guaranteed by the reach-avoid set arising from the barrier construction.

Gradient-Flow Methods for Bilevel Optimisation

With Mayank Baranwal, IIT Bombay. Undergraduate Research Award, 2025.

Applies the Polyak-Lojasiewicz inequality to the lower-level function of a continuous bilevel problem with a non-convex upper level, guaranteeing asymptotic convergence under minimal assumptions. The method uses a hypergradient estimator with Tikhonov regularisation to avoid singularity in the Hessian inverse, and a projection operator to keep gradients bounded.

Research

Lane-Less Autonomous Driving for Indian RoadsM.Tech thesis

Prof. Parthib Khound, IIT Bombay. Funded by Honda Cars India Limited.

The work has two halves. The first builds a faithful re-simulation of dense lane-less traffic, because models such as IDM and MOBIL are calibrated on traffic with fundamentally different structure, so the testbed has to be validated against real trajectory data before any controller is trusted inside it. The second is a planning and control pipeline for the ego vehicle, currently an MPC framework combined with a repulsive-potential lookahead planner whose formulation borrows the discounting idea from reinforcement learning. Every vehicle uses a kinematic bicycle model, and clearance is computed with exact rotated-rectangle geometry rather than centre to centre distance.

Fixed-Time Convergent Neural CBF-CLF Controller

Prof. Shishir Kolathaya, Stochastic Robotics Lab, IISc Bangalore.

A hybrid architecture in which a neural control Lyapunov function represents the minimum time to go toward the goal, and a single network learns obstacle avoidance in coordinates taken relative to each obstacle, which makes it scalable across state dimension and across the number of obstacles. Individual barriers are combined with a compositional soft minimum. Convergence is fixed time rather than asymptotic, so manoeuvres complete within a bounded horizon without violating safety.

Vision-Based Unmanned Aerial Manipulator Control

Prof. Lin Zhao, CORE Lab, National University of Singapore.

A three degree of freedom arm integrated on an S500 airframe with joint control over ROS and MAVROS. Several 6D pose estimation pipelines were compared, including FoundationPose, SAM-6D, EfficientPose and YOLOv5-6D, and FoundationPose was deployed for real-time tracking from RGB-D. Trajectory tracking under disturbance during high-speed pick-up used a hybrid MPC and L1 adaptive controller to absorb uncertainty in the model parameters. Closed-loop grasping was demonstrated both in PX4-Gazebo and on hardware.

Adversarial Training for Ergodic Reward

Prof. Dominik Baumann, Aalto University.

A Wasserstein GAN framework for reward transformation in non-ergodic environments, so that standard reinforcement learning algorithms which maximise long-term reward can be applied directly. A generator transforms the time-average rewards and a discriminator evaluates them against ensemble averages, with discriminator accuracy near 0.5 indicating successful training.

Projects

Fault-Tolerant Control under Single-Rotor Failure2nd, Inter-IIT Tech Meet

Inter-IIT Tech Meet, 2024.

Nonlinear dynamic inversion with feedback linearisation for a quadrotor that loses one rotor, with a modified two-stage Kalman filter detecting the failure from the onboard IMU and GPS alone and adapting the control effectiveness matrix within 0.03 seconds. Validated in Gazebo and on hardware using only the PX4 flight controller stack, with no external computation and no additional sensors. In the course of this work we found an error in the proof underlying the reference paper by Sun and Scaramuzza, rewrote it, and had the correction confirmed by the original author.

Thanopod, Two-Wheeled Self-Balancing Robot

Team genSUS, IIT Bombay. Robofest Gujarat 4.0, GUJCOST.

Motor dynamics were simulated to model the nonlinear system, and an LQR controller was designed for stability, estimation and precise motion control. A cascaded PID loop maintained balance while tracking a curvilinear path, with computer vision and a Stanley controller handling line detection and tracking.

Recursive Partitioning for Single-SKU Palletising

Robotics R&D Intern, ABB Innovation Centre, Bangalore.

A two-phase recursive partitioning algorithm for single-SKU palletising, a variant of the 2D bin packing problem, implemented in .NET C#. A mixed-SKU pattern generator was built in four variants to account for gripper safety margins, placement error and stability in transit, with backtracking for feasibility checks.
